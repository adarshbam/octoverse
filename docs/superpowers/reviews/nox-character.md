## Whole-character critique (2026-10-24)

After eyes v4, the owner sent a ten-point critique of the whole character, with high-quality versions of the storyboard panels (`docs/design/references/Nox/`). The eyes v4 question got no direct verdict; the critique asks to preserve "Nox's adorable facial identity, large expressive eyes". The critique, in short:

1. Cheeks look like separate rounded attachments; integrate them into the head silhouette.
2. Arms appear to emerge from behind the face; give all eight a unified central base.
3. Arms are too thin and ribbon-like; thicken by about 15–20%, fuller bases, gradual taper.
4. Curves are mathematically smooth; add asymmetry, twists, natural bends.
5. Suckers are glowing dots; make concave cups with raised rims, varied sizes, wet highlights.
6. The skin looks like rubber or plastic; make it wet, slimy and gelatinous, with glossy specular reflections.
7. The surface is too smooth; add fine bumps, translucent patches, subtle pigmentation.
8. The head is nearly a perfect sphere; make it more organic, with a smooth transition into a rounded central body.
9. Arms look layered, not connected; add occlusion and contact shadows, and shade foreground and background arms differently.
10. Gradients give brightness, not depth; layer purple tones with warm reddish subsurface light and small irregular wet reflections.

Priorities, quoted: "Anatomically connected cheeks, thicker organic tentacles, realistic suction cups, and especially wet, slimy, translucent skin with convincing 3D depth." The pasted prompt also said "Keep the existing SVG-based architecture"; the build is the 3D one the owner chose on 2026-10-23, so the existing 3D architecture is kept and only Nox changes.

### Glow and material v2 (2026-10-24)

Capture: `assets/glow-v2*.png`. No direct verdict; the owner sent a final-polish brief. It asks to "preserve Nox's identity, cuteness, proportions, facial expression, and overall silhouette, but fix the remaining flaws in material, color, lighting, anatomy, tentacle posing, and environmental integration". The twelve points, in short:

1. Less plastic shine: gloss "more controlled, natural, and selective".
2. Body colour: "a richer, deeper violet-blue base with better magenta transitions".
3. Underside: "soft subsurface glow, not a flat red/orange fill".
4. Lighting: "stronger form lighting, better shadow wrapping, clearer volume, and better contrast".
5. Glow: "a thin crisp luminous edge/rim light and a clean neon border, with less foggy airbrushed aura".
6. Skin: "moist, slimy, slightly translucent, delicate micro-variation, not noisy".
7. Arm anatomy: "better taper, more natural thickness transitions, clearer muscle flow, less tube-like".
8. Arm posing: bending "along the full length with multiple bend points".
9. Suckers: "more integrated, dimensional, and natural in spacing, size, and embedding".
10. Cheek-to-head and arm-root transitions: "anatomically smooth and unified".
11. Environment: "bounce light, reflected color, edge tinting, and atmospheric blending".
12. Depth: "better overlap, occlusion, silhouette clarity, and dimensional form separation".

The brief also asks to go "better than the reference", with "one strong key light, one crisp rim light, and one subtle warm underglow", better violet–magenta–coral harmony, and slightly sharper facial rendering. Constraints: no redesign, keep the cute identity, and do not reduce the glow energy.

### Glow and material v3 (2026-10-24)

Capture: `assets/glow-v3*.png` (full, left, right, head, body strips against the HQ panel and glow v2; cups close-up; review page suckers view; motion sheet; orbit). Verdict: approved. The owner wrote "Approve glow and material v3".

How v3 answers the brief:

- **Lighting hierarchy (4, 11 and "better than the reference"):** one key light from the upper left with soft wrap, then magenta light scattered at the terminator, the red band's subsurface glow, cool bounce from the water above and warm from below, a subtle warm underglow, surroundings reflected at grazing angles, and depth haze behind the body. The arms are rebuilt on it; the cups share the same lights. Stage values live in `config/lighting.ts`.
- **Plastic shine (1):** the broad sheen and the second top light are gone; gloss stays in wet patches, droplets and glints. The wet patches now have crisp, anti-aliased edges.
- **Colour (2, 3):** deeper violet body (#640a90) with a magenta terminator (#a8085a) and a red underside that glows up into the body instead of a flat fill. Arm colours track the HQ arm profile.
- **Arm anatomy (7):** the sucker side is flattened to 86% of the radius; the back stays round.
- **Suckers (9):** shallow coral discs sunk into the skin, each with a red slit along the arm instead of a round hole. Each sits on the skin itself, levelled to the skin under its edges and bent across and along the arm so it hugs it. Each casts a soft contact shadow onto the band, deeper away from the key light. Spacing varies by up to 9%. In tight curls, where the skin is squeezed, cups are squashed along the arm only when they would otherwise overlap. Cups seen edge-on flatten so they do not break the outline.
- **Transitions (10):** the cheek lobes blend into the head over a wider band (0.05 to 0.08); arm highlights, rim and saturation fade out where the arms rise from the neck.
- **Face (sharpen slightly):** the face texture is sampled one mip finer.
- **Skin (6):** the micro-bumps fade out wherever they would loom large on screen (up close, or along an arm seen at a grazing angle), where they smeared the highlights into blurry dashes.

Checks: suite 108/108, typecheck clean, `check_stage` PASS, no shader errors. Known gaps: the last cup at each tip can hang past the rounded end; at a steep 70-degree orbit an arm seen end-on still shows a large pale highlight (from v2); the HQ dome is lighter blue with more visible texture than ours; the lava environment is weaker than the painting's (stage work, not this task). Frame rate on the Intel UHD read ~19-32 fps at DPR 1 under a busy machine (v2 measured ~41 under light load); an A/B of the new cup-shadow pass was within noise.

### Motion v1 (2026-10-25)

Capture: `assets/motion-v1.webm` (10 s, `node scripts/motion-capture.mjs`: idle 4 s with the pointer away, then reach right, reach left and hover the face) and `assets/motion-v1-sheet.png` (12 frames, 0.83 s apart). Verdict: approved. The owner wrote "Approve motion v1".

Before review, the recording showed three motion defects, now fixed:

- **Twitching reaches:** the reach solver flipped which way a joint turned when the target lay almost behind it, and re-solved from scratch each frame, so a reaching arm jumped up to ~9 px in a frame. Joints now ease off near that point, and the arm follows the solver through a fast spring (~0.25 s).
- **Cups popping on moving arms:** each cup's fit to the skin could switch between two answers as an arm moved. Cups now find the skin by walking along it from their centre, so they follow the arm smoothly.
- **Arms folding into the face:** an arm kept following the pointer back toward the head and looped beside the cheek. Arms now let go of a pointer near the head; hovering the face only makes Nox smile.

Personality values are unchanged: blinks every 2.6-7 s (20% double), gaze speed 16, head 3.2, sway 0.5, curl 1.1, float 0.045 over 6.2 s, breath 4.6 s. Known gaps: reaches are gentle (the arm leans toward the pointer rather than stretching to it); breathing and float are subtle at stage size.

### Technology orbs v2 (2026-10-25)

Capture: `assets/orbs-v2.png`, `assets/orbs-v2-strip.png`, `assets/orbs-v2-zoom.png`, `assets/orbs-v2-compare.png`, `assets/orb-one-v2-compare.png`, `assets/orbs-v2-transition.webm` (sheet `assets/orbs-v2-transition-sheet.png`). Verdict: review-ready.

- **Sharp, concentrated specular arcs:** replaced the soft wide glint with crisp near-white highlights placed along curved rim arcs (`band * arcs`), matching the concentrated glints of the painting without threshold bloom hazing the glass.
- **Steep Fresnel & dark interior:** Fresnel curve steepened (`pow(1.0 - ndv, 7.0)`), keeping the sphere center dark and clear (linear `0.006` base tint) while the edge ring illuminates intensely.
- **Opaque, vivid logos:** scale enlarged to 2.1x; logos drawn with boosted saturation in sRGB (`s * 1.3 + 0.2` so muted icons like Node reach the painting's vivid neon); letter counter holes filled (HTML, CSS, TS, JS) matching the painting.
- **Localized reflections & colored outer halo:** each orb carries an additive outer halo shell (`scale 1.16`) softly glowing in the logo's own hue; glass reflections mix the logo tint with environment gradients (warm lava orange below, purple above, cool blue on the left).
- **Substantial body:** interior alpha raised to 0.72 (`clamp(0.72 + fresnel * 0.4 + shine, 0.0, 1.0)`), holding background tentacle details back so orbs stand out crisp and clean.

Checks: suite 160/160 tests PASS, `npm run typecheck` clean, `check_stage` panel 2 and panel 3 PASS.
